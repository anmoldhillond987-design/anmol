The practical bits

Before we

Who do you typically work with?+

Founders, small marketing teams, and ambitious brands that have a strong product and want marketing with more intention. I work across e-commerce, SaaS, consumer tech, and culture-led businesses.

What does a project together look like?+

We start with a focused discovery sprint, align on the opportunity and success metric, then move into a clear delivery plan. Engagements can be a one-off strategy sprint or ongoing monthly partnership.

How much do you charge?+

Most strategy projects begin at $2,500, with ongoing partnerships scoped around the pace and people involved. I will always recommend the smallest useful engagement first.

Do you work with in-house teams?+

Absolutely. I often plug into an existing team as the strategic and creative partner who helps connect the dots, build momentum, and leave the team stronger.
